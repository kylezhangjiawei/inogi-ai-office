items.reduce(
    (summary, item) => ({
      amount: summary.amount + item.qty * item.unitPrice,

orderedTotal > 0 ? Math.min(100, Math.round((receivedTotal / orderedTotal) * 100)) : 0

同单位超收抵消另一行缺收
输入：[{"id":"line-a","qty":10,"receivedQty":20,"unit":"件","unitPrice":1},{"id":"line-b","qty":10,"receivedQty":0,"unit":"件","unitPrice":1}]

当前计算显示100%，但line-b仍为0/10，line-a为20/10超收。不能将超收作为其他物料已到数量。

不同单位累加为整单比例
输入：[{"id":"line-kit","qty":1,"receivedQty":1,"unit":"套","unitPrice":1},{"id":"line-meter","qty":100,"receivedQty":0,"unit":"米","unitPrice":1}]
实际汇总：{"amount":101,"received":1,"ordered":101}
实际到货进度：1%
当前计算将1套与100米合为101，并显示1/101、1%。API已有行单位，不能将不同单位数值作为同一数量汇总。
